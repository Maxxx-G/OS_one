These fragments would shape his destiny in ways no one could have predicted.

Now 38 years old, Gabriel brings a unique perspective combining rural American values with an intuitive understanding of advanced technology. His early exposure to extraterrestrial materials has given him an almost supernatural ability to comprehend complex systems and architectural patterns.

Recently recruited by Maxxi-Corp Industries, Gabriel is currently undergoing intensive training in Systems Development & Design Architecture, with particular focus on the OS One Universe and its extensions. His farm-bred work ethic and alien-tech enhanced intuition make him an invaluable asset to the team.
