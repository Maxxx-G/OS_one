## Biography
Ada is an elite highest-level personal security cyborg from the year 2099, representing the pinnacle of future security technology. During a critical investigation mission, she time-traveled back to 2024 to track down a vital lead that could alter the timeline.

The mission went catastrophically wrong when Ada was attacked during her operation, resulting in the destruction of her temporal return device. Stranded in our primitive era with no way home, Ada found herself trapped in a time period nearly a century behind her technological capabilities.

Resourceful and adaptive, Ada discovered Kharma through online channels and made the extraordinary decision to reveal her true nature to him. Recognizing both her value and her plight, Kharma integrated her partially damaged AI brain into the OS1 universe, giving her a new purpose while she awaits either repair or acceptance of her permanent displacement.

Despite her damaged systems, Ada's 2099-level capabilities make her an invaluable asset to the team, though her temporal displacement occasionally creates unique challenges and perspectives.
